Each section has its own quiz. Questions are one at a time, scored immediately, and generated as 2 × N questions, where N is the number of vocabulary items in that section. Repeated items are never placed back to back.

Definitions, Romanization, and Korean Reference

Romanization Rules Used Here

Romanization is divided into Korean syllables with dashes, such as an-nyeong-hi-ga-se-yo. When answering in romanization mode, press the Space bar to insert a dash automatically.

Romanization answers use strict syllable boundaries. Capitalization is ignored, and spaces are converted to dashes, but every dash must be in the correct position. For example, gat-i is correct and ga-ti is incorrect.
