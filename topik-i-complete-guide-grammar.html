Complete Guide to the TOPIK I · Grammar

The 256 grammar bundles of the eight one-week workbooks that go with Complete Guide to the TOPIK I (3rd edition) — Part 1, Part 2 and the two mock tests, each in a listening and a reading book — with the book’s meaning, formation, example, dialogue and comparison note. Each book has eight question types, and each type is one unit of four bundles. Group them by unit, by book, or by question type across the books. The quiz asks the workbooks’ own 1280 answer-keyed questions, and cards built from every bundle.
