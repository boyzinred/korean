Grammar 1: 예요 / 이에요
Rule

N예요 / 이에요 (To be)

The copula 예요/이에요 is used to indicate 'to be'.

• If the noun does not have a final consonant (받침 X), attach 예요.
• If the noun has a final consonant (받침 O), attach 이에요.

Examples: 제니퍼예요 (Jennifer), 팀이에요 (Tim), 가수예요 (singer), 학생이에요 (student).

Grammar 2: 은 / 는
Rule

N은 / 는 (Topic Particle)

The particles 은 and 는 are used as subject or topic markers.

• If the noun does not have a final consonant (받침 X), attach 는.
• If the noun has a final consonant (받침 O), attach 은.

Examples: 저는 (I), 선생님은 (teacher), 피비는 (Phoebe), 학생은 (student).
