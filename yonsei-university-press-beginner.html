Tick one box on each side for a run that always goes the same way. Tick more than one and the run mixes directions, and every question says what to answer with.

Typed test: type each answer. A word is checked for you; a definition, usage pattern, expression or sentence is checked word for word (spaces and punctuation aside). Enter checks and Enter again moves on; a miss shows the book’s answer beside yours, and Count as correct takes an answer that says the same thing. Handwriting test: write the answer in 한국어 with a finger or a pen, reveal it, and mark yourself. Speaking test: say it in 한국어, reveal it, and mark yourself. Handwriting and speaking always answer in 한국어, so their questions come from the English side (usage is 한국어 on both sides already).
