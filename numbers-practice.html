Korean Number Systems
The core decision Native or Sino-Korean?

Korean uses two number systems. The number itself is not chosen randomly: the situation or counter usually determines which system you need.

Native Korean Common for counting things with counters, people, animals, age with 살 , clock hours with 시 , durations in 시간 , and occurrences with 번 .
Sino-Korean Common for money, calendar dates, years, minutes and seconds, floors, school grades, percentages, measurements, phone/serial digits, mathematics, and large values.
Mixed situations are normal A time such as 3:20 mixes systems: 세 시 이십 분 — native for the hour, Sino-Korean for the minutes.
Native Korean 1–99 and counter forms

Sino-Korean Digits, place values, and large-number grouping

The basic digits are 일 1, 이 2, 삼 3, 사 4, 오 5, 육 6, 칠 7, 팔 8, 구 9 . Zero is usually 영 , while 공 is very common when reading phone numbers and other identifiers.

1,000 천 2,026 = 이천이십육 2×1,000 + 2×10 + 6
10,000 만 12,500 = 만 이천오백 Korean begins a new large group at 10,000
100,000,000 억 100,000,000 = 일억 억 begins the next 10,000-based large group
1,000,000,000,000 조 1,000,000,000,000 = 일조 조 is another 10,000-fold step after 억

Before 십, 백, 천 , a leading 1 is normally omitted: 10 = 십, 100 = 백, 1,000 = 천. The value 10,000 is normally 만 , but larger group units keep 일 when the group itself is exactly one: 100,000,000 = 일억 , 1,000,000,000,000 = 일조 .

100 and above: native Korean numbers normally stop at 99. Large counted quantities therefore use Sino-Korean values, e.g. 백 명 (100 people), 천 개 (1,000 items).
Time A common mixed system
Hours use native Korean 1:00 한 시 · 2:00 두 시 · 3:00 세 시 · 4:00 네 시 · 12:00 열두 시.
Minutes and seconds use Sino-Korean 3:20 = 세 시 이십 분 · 7:45 = 일곱 시 사십오 분.
Hour durations also use native Korean 2 hours = 두 시간, while 20 minutes = 이십 분.

Dates and special forms Calendar numbers are Sino-Korean

Years, months, and dates use Sino-Korean numbers. Two month names have standard shortened pronunciations/spellings:

June 6월 is 유월 , not 육월.
October 10월 is 시월 , not 십월.
Month duration 3 months = 삼 개월 ; the native alternative 세 달 is also common, but this exercise uses 개월 when testing Sino-Korean month duration.

Decimals and signs Measurements, decimal points, and temperature

Decimals normally use Sino-Korean numbers with 점 for the decimal point, then read each following digit separately. Negative temperatures can use 영하 ; positive temperatures are often simply stated with 도.
