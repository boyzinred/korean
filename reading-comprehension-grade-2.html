Korean Reading Comprehension · Grade 2

깊이 읽고 답해요

Ten original grade 2 Korean passages — longer paragraphs, dialogue, past tense, a folk tale, a fable, and two nonfiction pieces. Each has ten multiple-choice questions with four answers, covering the main idea, the details, the characters, the setting, the order of events, cause and effect, and what is real versus make-believe. Read the passage and the questions in Hangul, in romanization, or in English, and click any underlined word for a three-level popover.
