Complete Guide to the TOPIK I · Vocabulary

Every word in the eight one-week workbooks that go with Complete Guide to the TOPIK I (3rd edition): Part 1 and Part 2, and the two mock tests, each in a listening and a reading book. Each book has eight question types, and each type is one unit: its 1536 headwords (24 a unit, with the book’s expression, example sentence and translation), then the 767 words of its 유형별 보충 어휘 list. Group them by unit, filed under the book’s own parts, or by part of speech. Star what you know, and test yourself on the translation, on each example sentence with the word taken out, or on the workbook’s own 문맥 어휘 fill-ins.
